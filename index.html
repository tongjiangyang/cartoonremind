卡通提醒小助手 · 帮助与支持

遇到问题或有建议，欢迎发邮件到 755080021@qq.com，我们会尽快回复。

收不到提醒？

请在 iPhone「设置 → 通知 → 卡通提醒小助手」中确认已允许通知。如果超过 36 小时没有打开 App，后续提醒会在下次打开时重新排定，建议每天打开一次。

如何恢复购买？

在 App 的会员页点击「恢复购买」，并确认使用的是当初购买时的 Apple ID。

如何取消订阅？

打开 iPhone「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」，选择「卡通提醒小助手」后取消。取消后，会员权益会保留到当前周期结束。

专注时能不能锁住手机？

iOS 不允许 App 阻止退出。如需真正锁定，可使用系统「引导式访问」：设置 → 辅助功能 → 引导式访问，开启后在专注界面连按三下侧边键。

Toon Reminder · Help & Support

Questions or suggestions? Email 755080021@qq.com and we'll get back to you soon.

Not getting reminders?

Make sure notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Toon Reminder. Reminders are scheduled 36 hours ahead; if you don't open the app for longer than that, later reminders are rescheduled the next time you open it, so opening it once a day is recommended.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap "Restore Purchases" on the membership page and make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, choose Toon Reminder and cancel. You keep membership until the end of the current period. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Can I lock my phone while focusing?

iOS doesn't let apps stop you from leaving. To truly lock the screen, use Guided Access: Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access, then triple-click the side button on the focus screen.
