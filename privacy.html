卡通提醒小助手 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日　·　最近更新：2026 年 9 月 24 日
简要说明：本 App 不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何个人信息。你的提醒、打卡记录和专注记录只保存在你自己的设备上。App 内不含广告、统计分析或第三方追踪代码，也不会连接开发者的服务器。

一、我们在设备上保存的信息

为了让 App 正常工作，以下内容会保存在你设备本地的 App 存储空间内，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方：

  • 你创建的提醒：名称、提示文字、时间间隔、声音 / 震动 / 闹钟开关等设置；
  • 打卡统计：最近 30 天每条提醒的完成次数、连续打卡天数；
  • 专注模式设置与记录：专注时长、自定义文字、所选背景音、完成次数与累计专注时长；
  • App 偏好设置（例如音量、免打扰时段）。

删除 App 即会同时删除以上全部数据。如果你开启了 iCloud 或电脑备份，这些数据可能会随系统备份一起保存，这由 Apple 的备份服务处理，开发者无法访问。

二、系统权限

  • 通知：用于在你设定的时间推送提醒。通知由 iOS 在本机排定和展示。
  • 闹钟（AlarmKit，iOS 26 及以上）：仅在你把某条提醒设为「闹钟级」时使用，用于到点全屏响铃。

你可以随时在 iPhone「设置」中关闭这些权限，关闭后相应的提醒方式将不可用，但不影响其他功能。

三、购买与订阅

「专注会员」的季度订阅和永久买断均通过 Apple 的 App Store 内购完成。付款、账单和订阅管理由 Apple 处理，开发者不会获得你的银行卡、支付账户或 Apple ID 信息。App 只会通过 Apple 提供的 StoreKit 接口读取经过验证的购买记录，以判断是否解锁会员功能。

Apple 对这些信息的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。你可以在 iPhone「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中查看或取消订阅。

四、我们不做的事

  • 不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片或设备标识符；
  • 不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不进行跨 App 追踪；
  • 不接入第三方统计、广告或崩溃分析 SDK；
  • 不向任何第三方出售或共享你的信息。

五、背景音

专注模式中的三种自然环境音随 App 一起安装，在本机播放，不需要联网下载，也不使用麦克风。

六、儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括未满 14 周岁的儿童。未成年人购买会员前，请在监护人同意和指导下进行。

七、政策更新

如果今后 App 的数据处理方式发生变化（例如增加联网功能），我们会先更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期；如有重大变化，会在 App 内提示。

八、联系我们

如果你对本政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至：755080021@qq.com

Toon Reminder Privacy Policy

Effective: September 24, 2026 · Last updated: September 25, 2026
In short: Toon Reminder does not collect, upload or sell any personal information. Your reminders, check-ins and focus records stay on your device. The app has no ads, analytics or third-party tracking code and does not connect to the developer's servers.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following locally on your device. It is never sent to the developer or any third party:

  • Reminders you create: name, message, interval, and sound / vibration / alarm settings;
  • Check-in stats: completions per reminder for the last 30 days and your day streak;
  • Focus settings and records: duration, custom text, chosen sound, number of sessions and total focus time;
  • App preferences (such as volume and quiet hours).

Deleting the app deletes all of this data. If you use iCloud or computer backups, the data may be included in your system backup, which is handled by Apple and not accessible to the developer.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications: used to deliver reminders at the times you set. Notifications are scheduled and shown by iOS on your device.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit, iOS 26 and later): only used when you make a reminder an alarm, so it rings full-screen on time.

You can turn these off anytime in iPhone Settings; the related reminder style will stop working but nothing else is affected.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

The quarterly membership and the lifetime purchase are handled through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment, billing and subscription management are handled by Apple; the developer never receives your card, payment account or Apple Account details. The app only reads verified purchase records through Apple's StoreKit to decide whether membership features are unlocked.

Apple's handling of this information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. You can view or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers;
  • We don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across apps;
  • We don't include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

5. Sounds

The three focus soundscapes are bundled with the app and play on your device. No download or microphone access is needed.

6. Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Minors should purchase a membership only with a parent's or guardian's consent.

7. Changes

If the way the app handles data ever changes (for example, adding online features), we will update this page and its effective date first, and let you know in the app for significant changes.
